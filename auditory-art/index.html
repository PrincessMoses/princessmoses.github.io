Super Music 2

This my second project in the "Super Music" series. It was important to me that I only worked on this project when I was heavily emotionally charged. I wanted to expand on the world I made in "Super Music" and develop different sections of the song that jump sporadically between each other. This makes the journey a short-lived sequence of leaps; a sad acceptance of conclusion.

Super Music

My first major musical project to find itself a stable, breathing identity in my mind. Super Music is a wandering song of songs. I like to listen to this when I feel stuck; it takes me to many different places and teaches me many different things.

Fun Magic Mystic

Fun Magic Mystic was my first project under the Princess Moses alias. This short collage of miscellaneous music is an amalgam of multiple sources of inspiration. The purpose of the process was to take it as unseriously as possible, resulting in several overwhelming and unrelated instrumentals.
